# Conversion sections

A landing page is an argument: **problem → why this works → proof → offer → no risk → act.** Every section moves the visitor one step.

## 1. Brief first
Unknown → ask or mark `[TODO]`; never invent: product, price, variants · one primary persona and the moment they feel the pain · the mechanism (why it works) · real proof · guarantee, shipping, returns · top 5 objections.

## 2. Order by awareness
| Visitor arrives... | Order |
|---|---|
| **Pain-aware** (ad about the pain) | Hero(pain→outcome) · Problem · Mechanism · Comparison · Proof · Offer · Guarantee · FAQ · Final CTA |
| **Solution-aware** (searching the category) | Hero(why us) · Proof strip · Comparison · Mechanism · Offer · Guarantee · FAQ · Final CTA |
| **Product-aware** (retargeting) | Hero(offer) · Offer · Proof · Guarantee · FAQ · Final CTA |
Long pages get a sticky CTA after the hero leaves the screen.

## 3. Section library
- **Hero**: headline = outcome, not product. Sub = mechanism + who it's for. One primary CTA. Visual showing the key feature. Micro-trust line under the CTA (only if real). All above the fold at 390px.
- **Proof strip**: media logos, customer count, rating. Real numbers only.
- **Problem**: 3 short pains in the customer's words (from reviews). Empathy, no fear-mongering.
- **Mechanism**: 3 steps or features, feature → benefit. Diagram / exploded view beats text.
- **Comparison**: us vs the generic alternative (not a named competitor), 4-6 honest rows.
- **Finder / quiz**: 2-4 questions → recommended variant → product card + CTA.
- **Social proof**: real reviews with name/initial + context. None yet → `[TODO: real reviews]` block; never fake testimonials, even as a demo.
- **Offer**: bundles with the middle one highlighted, unit price, what's included, full final price (Israel: incl. VAT). Urgency only if true.
- **Guarantee**: actual terms in plain words, big, next to the offer.
- **FAQ**: 5-8 answers to the brief's objections.
- **Final CTA**: outcome + offer + guarantee in one block.

## 4. Hebrew copy rules
- Gender-neutral without slashes: plural imperative ("הזמינו"), infinitive ("להזמנה"), noun CTAs ("לרכישה"). Consistent across the page.
- Warm, direct, short sentences; no translated marketing-English.
- CTA = action + result ("להזמנה עם משלוח חינם"), not "שלח" / "לחץ כאן".
- One price convention (`₪149` or `149 ₪`); isolate mixed Hebrew/Latin/number strings (`<bdi>` / `unicode-bidi: isolate`).
- Health products: comfort and support, not cures; any claim needs a source on the page.
- Israel basics: full price, clear return/cancellation terms for online purchases, business details in the footer. Tell the user to verify current consumer-protection requirements; don't give legal advice.

## 5. Self-check
- [ ] Hero alone answers what, for whom, why better, what to do (5-second test).
- [ ] One job per section; cut the rest.
- [ ] Every objection answered.
- [ ] No invented numbers, reviews, awards or urgency.
- [ ] One primary CTA wording, repeated; secondary actions quieter.
- [ ] Gender-neutral, consistent Hebrew.
