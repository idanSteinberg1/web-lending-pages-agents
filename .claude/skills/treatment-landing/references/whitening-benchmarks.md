# Teeth whitening benchmarks (researched 2026-09-28)

What each site does well, so agents can borrow patterns, not copy layouts or copy text.

## Clinics (in-office whitening)
| Site | Worth borrowing |
|---|---|
| [Dntl Bar, NYC](https://www.dntlbar.com/teeth-whitening/) | Outcome + time in the headline ("2–6 shades in 30–90 minutes"), phone as secondary CTA, "judgement-free care" tone, financing mention |
| [The Smile Studio, Phoenix](https://www.thesmilestudioaz.com/teeth-whitening) | Question-shaped section headings (candidate, what to expect, how long, how long it lasts, maintenance, cost); comfort amenities as a differentiator; contraindications stated |
| [London Teeth Whitening](https://www.london-teeth-whitening.co.uk/) | Price anchor in the H1 ("from £395"), 3-option comparison (in-chair / home / combined), 5 before-after cases before the FAQ, 5+ booking CTAs. Their urgency tactics ("slots go fast") are a pattern to avoid unless true |
| [DentAkademi, London: "Honest limits"](https://dentakademi.co.uk/teeth-whitening-london/) | Best-in-class honesty: what whitening can't do, published fees, "what to ask a clinic" checklist, red-flags list, clinician named with registration number, low-cost online consultation as entry |
| [Whites Dental, Waterloo](https://www.whitesdental.co.uk/enlighten-teeth-whitening-in-london-waterloo/) · [NW1 Dental Care](https://www.nw1dentalcare.co.uk/enlighten-teeth-whitening-london/) · [Wimpole Dental Office](https://www.wimpoledentaloffice.com/teeth-whitening-london) · [The Cosmetic Dental Gallery](https://thecosmeticdentalgallery.co.uk/cosmetic-dentistry/teeth-whitening/) · [London City Smiles](https://www.londoncitysmiles.com/teeth-whitening-islington/) · [teethwhitening.london](https://www.teethwhitening.london/) | Price in page title, system named (Enlighten), gallery pages |
| [Complete Smiles: shade simulator](https://completesmilesbv.com.au/teeth-whitening-shade-simulator/) | Interactive shade preview on a standard shade scale, with an explicit "preview, not a guaranteed outcome" disclaimer and consultation CTA |

## Gaps we can beat
Most clinic pages show results as text only or as a static grid, bury prices, and never say what whitening can't do. An interactive before/after with honest captions, a shade strip, a candidate check and a clear "from" price already puts a page ahead of most of these.
