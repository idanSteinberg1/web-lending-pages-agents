# Treatment landing pages

A treatment page sells a result the visitor can't try before buying, on their body, from a stranger. It converts when it answers three fears in order: **"Will it work on me?" → "Is it safe / who does it?" → "What will it cost and what happens next?"** Benchmarks behind this skill are in `references/whitening-benchmarks.md`.

## 1. Section order (default)
| # | Section | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero: outcome headline + result visual + one CTA + location/hours microtrust | "This is for me, and it's nearby" |
| 2 | Results: real before/after (interactive) | "It works on people like me" |
| 3 | How it works: 3–5 steps with duration | "I know exactly what will happen" |
| 4 | Candidate check: suitable / not suitable / needs assessment | "Will it work on *me*?" (honest limits build trust) |
| 5 | Options comparison (if >1 method) | Self-segmentation: in-clinic vs home vs combined |
| 6 | Who treats you: named clinician, title, license number, photo | Safety + accountability |
| 7 | Price: "from ₪X", what's included, what changes the price | Removes the #1 reason people don't call |
| 8 | FAQ | Objections |
| 9 | Low-friction CTA: consultation / WhatsApp / callback | Next step that feels small |

Rules: primary CTA wording stays identical everywhere ("לתיאום טיפול"), visible every 1–2 screens, plus a sticky mobile CTA bar. Phone and WhatsApp are always one tap away.

## 2. Results (the heart of the page)
- **Interactive before/after slider** in or right after the hero, with a case switcher; each case auto-reveals on switch, autoplay stops on interaction, pause button present (see the Medical Place example pattern).
- Same angle, same light, same retractor, cropped to the treated area only (no faces). If "after" was shot before removing protective material (e.g. gingival barrier), **say so under the image**.
- Caption every case with what was done and how long, when known ("in-clinic, 1 session, 60 min").
- **Shade scale strip** (whitening-specific): a row of shade tabs from dark to light (e.g. VITA order A3.5 → A2 → A1 → B1) with the case's start and end marked. Never claim a guaranteed end shade.
- **Shade / result simulator** (optional signature): visitor picks their current shade and sees an *illustrative* lighter range. Mandatory disclaimer next to it: "המחשה בלבד. התוצאה בפועל תלויה במצב האמייל, בהרגלים ובשיטת הטיפול, ונקבעת בבדיקה."
- A gallery (rail or grid) of more cases below; 5–60 cases is normal on strong sites.

## 3. Honesty = conversion (the "honest limits" pattern)
Top-performing clinic pages state what the treatment **can't** do. It filters bad-fit leads and raises trust. For whitening:
- Doesn't change the colour of crowns, veneers, bonding or visible fillings (they may need replacing after whitening to match).
- Some intrinsic stains (e.g. medication-related, trauma-darkened single tooth, developmental) respond poorly and need assessment.
- Temporary sensitivity is common and manageable; sharp or lasting pain is not normal → call the clinic.
- Results are not permanent: typically months to a couple of years depending on habits (coffee, tea, wine, smoking); top-ups are normal.
- Not recommended during pregnancy/breastfeeding or with untreated decay/gum disease (clinic to confirm its own list).
Put these in the candidate-check section and the FAQ, in plain Hebrew, not in small print.

## 4. Claims discipline
- Every number needs a source the client can show: "up to X shades", "Y minutes", "Z% little or no sensitivity" → footnote with basis (e.g. manufacturer study, n, year) or remove.
- Prefer ranges and "typically" over absolutes. No "guaranteed", "permanent", "painless", "best in Israel".
- Name the system/brand only if the clinic really uses it (and follow the brand's usage rules).
- Before/afters must be of the clinic's own patients, with written consent, unedited except crop/rotation.
- Tell the user to check the advertising and professional rules that apply to the clinic (who may perform the treatment, product concentration limits, before/after rules). Don't assert specific regulations yourself unless you've verified them.

## 5. Trust elements (ranked by impact)
1. Named clinician with title and license/registration number + photo.
2. Real before/afters (above).
3. Real reviews with name/initial and source (Google rating embed if available).
4. Clinic photos: treatment room, the device, the team (not stock).
5. Technology/system used, with what it means for the patient (comfort, speed, sensitivity).
6. Address + map + hours in the hero microtrust and footer.

## 6. Pricing patterns
- Best: "from ₪X" + what's included (consultation, cleaning, number of sessions, home kit, follow-up) + "what changes the price".
- Launch offers: only with a real end date; show original and offer price; no fake countdowns.
- Low-friction entry: a paid or free short consultation, or "send a photo on WhatsApp for a first opinion".
- Comparison table when several options exist: method · sessions · time in clinic · typical result · maintenance · price.

## 7. FAQ bank (whitening; answer with the clinic's real details)
- למי הטיפול מתאים? ולמי לא?
- כמה זמן לוקח הטיפול, וכמה מפגשים צריך?
- כמה גוונים אפשר לצפות? (טווח, לא הבטחה)
- זה כואב? תהיה רגישות? מה עושים אם כן?
- כמה זמן התוצאה מחזיקה, ואיך שומרים עליה?
- מה עם כתרים, ציפויים או סתימות בשיניים הקדמיות?
- מה אסור לאכול ולשתות אחרי הטיפול, ולכמה זמן?
- מה ההבדל בין הלבנה בקליניקה לערכה ביתית?
- כמה זה עולה ומה כלול?
- מי מבצע את הטיפול?
- מה זה הכחול שרואים בתמונות? (מגן חניכיים)

## 8. Visual direction for clinical treatments
- Clean, bright, lots of air; one calm accent colour (e.g. turquoise/teal) with an ink-dark text colour. Avoid clinical cold blue + stock "smiling model" hero.
- Real clinic imagery > stock. Show hands, tools and the result, not generic faces.
- An editorial serif or a soft humanist sans for headlines makes it feel premium rather than hospital-like.
- One signature moment only: usually the before/after or shade experience.

## 9. DTC (home kit / product) variant
When the client sells a product rather than a clinic visit, add: bundles with a highlighted middle option, subscription (save % + free shipping, "pause/skip/cancel anytime"), satisfaction guarantee with real terms, ingredient education (e.g. sensitivity-friendly formulas), UGC videos, review count + rating, press logos only if real, a quiz that recommends a kit. Pair with `conversion-sections` and `micro-interactions` (cart patterns).

## 10. QA checklist (add to site-critic for treatment pages)
- [ ] Results visible within the first two screens on mobile.
- [ ] Every result image: consent confirmed, cropped to treated area, honest caption.
- [ ] "Can't do / not suitable" content present and in plain language.
- [ ] Every numeric claim has a source or is removed.
- [ ] Clinician named with credentials; address and hours visible.
- [ ] Price or "from" price present, or a clear reason why not + free/cheap consultation path.
- [ ] Phone/WhatsApp one tap away on mobile; sticky CTA works.
